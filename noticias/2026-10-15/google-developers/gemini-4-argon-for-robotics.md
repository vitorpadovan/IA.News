# Gemini 4 Argon for Robotics

Gemini 4 Argon is a general-purpose physical reasoning model designed to enhance workflows across the robotics stack with advanced capabilities in physical understanding, code generation, and visual-semantic reasoning. Developers can access and integrate Argon across Google's developer platforms—including Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, and Google Antigravity—to build and test sophisticated robotic applications. By leveraging simulation tools like MuJoCo and code-as-policies, engineers can seamlessly transition from real-world data or sketches to physically verified robot control and self-improvement workflows.
