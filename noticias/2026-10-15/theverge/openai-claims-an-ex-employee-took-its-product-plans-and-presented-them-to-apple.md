# OpenAI claims an ex-employee took its product plans and presented them to Apple

OpenAI claims a former employee took more than 100 files related to its product plans before joining Apple. In [a document filed on Wednesday](https://www.documentcloud.org/documents/28812737-openai-trade-secrets-claim/), OpenAI accuses the unnamed employee of sending this information to a personal account and then presenting it to Apple leadership, including the vice president of the company's operations in Asia.

The filing, [reported earlier by Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/openai-employee-who-left-apple-took-files-about-unreleased-device-openai-alleges-2026-10-14/), is tied to a legal battle between [Apple and OpenAI](https://www.theverge.com/tech/964843/apple-openai-lawsuit-wildest-claims) over trade secrets - but this time, OpenAI is doing the accusing.
